# 💡 Key Learning

A resume may already be strong, but ATS optimization helps align it with specific job requirements.

The biggest improvement came from:

✅ Better keyword alignment

✅ Improved project descriptions

✅ React-specific terminology

✅ Recruiter-friendly presentation

---

# 🚀 Biggest Takeaway

> ATS optimization is not about adding fake experience.
>
> It is about presenting existing skills and projects in a way that matches recruiter expectations and job requirements.
