# Day 2 - What Is Prompt Engineering

## 📅 Date
June 2, 2026

## 🎯 Objective

Today's goal was to understand Prompt Engineering and learn how the quality of a prompt directly affects the quality of AI-generated responses.

---

## 🤖 What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt Engineering is the process of writing clear, specific, and structured instructions for AI systems to get better results.

A well-written prompt provides context, objectives, constraints, and formatting requirements that help AI generate more accurate and useful outputs.

---

## 📝 Activity Performed

### 1. Lazy Prompt

**Prompt Used:**

> Create an image explaining Prompt Engineering

### Result

The output was simple and lacked detailed explanations, structure, and visual organization.

---

### 2. Engineered Prompt

**Prompt Used:**

> You are an AI educator explaining Prompt Engineering to complete beginners in simple terms. Explain what it is, why it matters, provide examples, and create a clear educational infographic with professional design.

### Result

The output was much more detailed, structured, visually appealing, and easier to understand.

---

## 🔍 Comparison

| Aspect | Lazy Prompt | Engineered Prompt |
|----------|------------|------------------|
| Clarity | Basic | Detailed |
| Structure | Limited | Well Organized |
| Examples | Few | Multiple |
| Visual Design | Generic | Professional |
| Learning Value | Moderate | High |

---

## 📚 Key Learnings

- Better prompts produce better outputs.
- Context helps AI understand the task.
- Specific instructions improve response quality.
- Structured prompts lead to structured answers.
- Prompt Engineering is an important skill for working effectively with AI.

---

## 🚀 Takeaway

This activity demonstrated that AI performance depends heavily on how instructions are written. Even a small improvement in prompt quality can significantly improve the final result.

**Lesson Learned:** Better Prompts = Better Results.
