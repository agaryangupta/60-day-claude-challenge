## 🎯 Objective

Today's goal was to understand how AI can be used to analyze and optimize resumes for ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) and recruiter readability.

---

## 🤖 What is an ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by companies to scan, filter, and rank resumes before they reach a recruiter.

A resume that is ATS-friendly has a higher chance of passing the initial screening process.

---

## 📝 Activity Performed

For today's challenge, I uploaded my resume to Claude and used an AI Resume Optimizer prompt.

The AI analyzed:

- ATS Compatibility
- Resume Structure
- Keyword Usage
- Readability
- Content Quality

It then generated recommendations and an optimized version of the resume.

---

## 📊 Observations

### What AI Improved

✅ ATS-friendly formatting

✅ Better keyword optimization

✅ Stronger action verbs

✅ Improved readability

✅ Reduced redundancy

### What I Learned

While AI provided useful suggestions, some sections of my original resume already communicated my projects and experiences more effectively.

This showed me that AI is a valuable assistant, but human review is still important.

---

## 💡 Biggest Insight

> AI can help improve a resume, but it should be used as a collaborator, not a replacement.

The best results come from combining AI recommendations with personal review and domain knowledge.
