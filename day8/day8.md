# 💡 Biggest Learning

Environmental data becomes far more useful when it is translated into practical health insights.

Most people know their city's AQI value.

Very few understand how that number affects:

- Daily activities
- Exercise performance
- Sleep quality
- Hair health
- Skin health

AI can bridge this gap by converting complex environmental information into actionable recommendations.

---

# 🔍 Observations

- Air quality can vary significantly even within nearby regions.
- Environmental health is influenced by multiple factors beyond AQI.
- Visualization makes environmental data easier to understand.
- Personalized insights improve user engagement.
