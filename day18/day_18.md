# Day 18 - Brain Dump Action Planner Custom Skill

## 📅 Date

June 18, 2026

---

# 🎯 Objective

Today's goal was to build a reusable AI workflow that transforms unstructured notes, brainstorming sessions, meeting discussions, and project conversations into a structured action plan.

The focus was on reducing information overload and converting messy inputs into actionable insights through a custom skill.

---

# 🧠 Custom Skill Created

## Skill Name

**brain-dump-action-planner**

### Purpose

Transform:

- Meeting Notes
- Brainstorming Sessions
- Voice Memo Transcripts
- Project Discussions
- Class Notes
- Random Brain Dumps

into structured dashboards containing:

- Summaries
- Action Items
- Open Questions
- Risks & Blockers
- Decisions
- Conflicts

# 🤖 Generated Dashboard Sections

### 📌 Summary

Provided a concise overview of the entire discussion and highlighted the most important topics.

### 💡 Key Takeaways

Identified major decisions and important discussion points.

### ✅ Action Items

Generated a structured task table with:

| Task | Owner | Deadline | Status |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Contact Sponsors | Aryan | Next Week | Pending |
| UI Mockups | Rahul | July 5 | Pending |
| Marketing Campaign | Team | July 10 | Pending |

---

### ❓ Open Questions

Highlighted unresolved topics:

- Should online participation be allowed?
- How should additional mentors be recruited?
- What is the final budget allocation?

---

### ⚠ Risks & Blockers

Detected:

- Sponsor confirmation delays
- Pending budget approval
- Mentor shortage
- Event logistics dependencies

---

### 🔄 Conflict Detection

Identified overlapping deadlines and dependencies that may affect project execution.

---

# 💡 Key Learnings

- AI can transform unstructured information into organized workflows.
- Structured dashboards improve decision-making and project tracking.
- Custom Skills eliminate repetitive prompting.
- Action-oriented outputs are often more valuable than raw summaries.
- AI can act as a productivity assistant for managing projects and meetings.

---

# 🚀 Biggest Takeaway

> The value of AI is not only in generating content but also in organizing information into clear, actionable plans.

Custom Skills help automate repetitive workflows and turn scattered thoughts into structured execution plans.
